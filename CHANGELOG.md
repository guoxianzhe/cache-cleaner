# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- Replace the circular desktop portrait with an original full-body transparent chibi and broom.
- Add idle blinking, working hand pose and smiling completion pose.
- Display custom companion images in full with their aspect ratio and transparency preserved.
- Resize the companion window and anchor the cleaning bubble to the whole character.
- Retain the square portrait only for the application icon.

## 0.3.0

- Clicking the avatar always starts a fresh scan and shows a compact speech bubble.
- Confirm cleanup directly with Clean, or dismiss with Later; no second modal.
- Add working pulse, animated popover and completion hop with Reduce Motion support.
- Keep the full directory breakdown and error report accessible through Details.

## 0.2.0

- Introduce the Cache-Chan identity and original chibi mascot.
- Replace the always-visible utility window with a draggable avatar companion.
- Add local custom avatars, center cropping, reset and persistent placement.
- Add an English cleaning panel, menu, About dialog and copyright footer.
- Include MIT licensing, artwork, project showcase and contributor guidance.
- Preserve the fixed cache scope, scan-first workflow and confirmation before deletion.

## 0.1.0

- Initial native macOS cache scanner and cleaner with isolated self-tests.
